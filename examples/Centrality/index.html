About centrality

Centrality algorithms score each account by its place in the network. The chart sizes and colours accounts by that score.

Degree counts the accounts each account sends money to or receives money from.

Closeness measures how few transfers separate each account from every other. It finds accounts that money reaches quickly.

Betweenness counts the shortest paths that run through each account. It finds the accounts that money flows through, such as clearing accounts.

PageRank weights incoming transfers by the score of the sender. It finds accounts that collect money from well connected payers.

EigenCentrality rewards links to accounts that are themselves well connected.

Transfer volumes draws each link wider the more transfers it carries.

3D view lays the network out in depth. Drag to rotate, right drag to pan, scroll to zoom, and click an account to bring it to the centre.
